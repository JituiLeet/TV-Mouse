# TV Mouse

[简体中文](./README.md) | [English](./README.en.md)

Control the mouse pointer on Android TV with a remote, and operate other apps just like a mouse.

## Features

- Floating mouse pointer: move with the directional keys, click with the confirm button, and long-press with a long press of the confirm button.
- After enabling "Swipe Interface Operation" in settings, you can control interface swiping.
- Support custom pointer appearances: built-in default appearance, and you can also import images and save them as new appearances.
- Support Chinese and English interfaces: follows the system language by default, and can also be switched manually.

## Usage

Open the app's main interface, grant the overlay permission and accessibility service permission, and then you can enable the mouse function.

After enabling, use the remote's directional keys to move the pointer, press the confirm button to click, and long-press the confirm button to perform a long-press action.

Long-press the Back button for 5 seconds to open settings. You can also directly enter custom appearance or language settings from the app's main interface.

After enabling "Swipe Interface Operation" in settings, you can control interface swiping. After enabling it, long-press the Back button for 2 seconds or turn it off in settings.

In the app's main interface or settings, you can hide or turn off the mouse pointer. Set the language and customize the appearance.

## Custom Appearance

In the "Custom Appearance" option in settings, import a `128 × 128` pixel PNG or JPG image. A transparent-background PNG is recommended.

After entering a name and saving, you can switch and use it in the appearance list.

## Compatibility

Supports Android 4.4 (API 19) to Android 14 (API 34)
